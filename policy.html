Privacy Policy

Fituous is made by Softklass, LLC. This is the whole policy, and it is short because there is not much to describe.

Children

Fituous is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We advise children under the age of 13 to not use this application.

Data Collection and Storage

We do not collect any data. The only data involved in this app is what you search for, and that is saved in the database on the device and later retrieved to populate your progress and daily stats. In addition, the only data that is stored, is stored on the local database. You can delete your data by deleting the app data. No data of any kind is collected or shared. The app reads HealthKit if you grant the permission, and uses the information to adjust your calorie allowance, and displays progression results based on that data. It is never shared with any third-party, and doesn't leave the application.

Health & safety

This app is in no way shape or form providing any health recommendations. It is simply a calorie app that provides your information in a graphical way so that you can make decisions. We take no responsibility for any decisions you make based on the information provided.

This app is simply designed to track your calories.
