Privacy Policy

Fituous is made by Softklass, LLC. This is the whole policy, and it is short because there is not much to describe.

Children

Fituous is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We advise children under the age of 13 to not use this application.

Data Collection and Storage

The data you search for and track is saved in the local database on your device and later retrieved to populate your progress and daily stats. All data is stored locally on your device. We do not share this data with any third-party. No data is transmitted to a server, it stays on your device.

Health/Fitness Integration

The app can read from your device's health system if you grant permission. On iOS, this is Apple Health (HealthKit). On Android, this is Health Connect. The app uses this information to adjust your calorie allowance and display progression results based on that data. This data is never shared with any third-party and doesn't leave the device. It is stored locally on your device and synchronized only through your device's native encrypted health system.

Data Deletion

You can delete your local data at any time:
  • iOS: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Fituous → Delete App Data (or uninstall the app)
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Fituous → Storage → Clear Data (or uninstall the app)

Camera Usage

The app requests camera access exclusively to scan food barcodes when identifying products. We do not access your photo library, capture photos, or transmit any camera data to our servers. All barcode scans are processed locally on your device.

Health & safety

This app is in no way shape or form providing any health recommendations. It is simply a calorie app that provides your information in a graphical way so that you can make decisions. We take no responsibility for any decisions you make based on the information provided.

This app is simply designed to track your calories.
